# Linc'well, Inc. (株式会社Linc'well)

> Linc'well is a Tokyo-based healthcare technology company founded in January 2018 that operates a
> healthcare platform connecting medical professionals with patients across online and offline care.
> Its stated mission is to take healthcare one step further through technology. The company runs three
> lines of business: a telehealth platform licensed to medical institutions, a clinic DX ("Smart Clinic")
> service that digitizes primary-care operations, and a healthcare e-commerce business covering
> cosmetics, contact lenses, OTC medicines, and online pharmacy dispensing. Linc'well operates the
> CLINIC FOR clinic brand. It is a private company backed by DCM Ventures.

This file was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Linc'well corporate

## Business lines

- [Telehealth Platform](https://linc-well.com/en/service/): Online medical consultation systems
  provided to medical institutions. Patients complete booking through consultation on web, smartphone,
  or tablet. Payment by credit card or pay-later; medication delivered or collected at a local pharmacy.
- [Smart Clinic (clinic DX)](https://linc-well.com/en/service/): Digitization of core clinic
  operations — appointment booking, medical interview/intake, payment, and medical record entry.
- [Healthcare EC](https://linc-well.com/en/service/): Online sale of self-medication products
  including cosmetics, contact lenses (controlled medical devices), and non-prescription medicines,
  plus prescription dispensing, online medication guidance, and rapid delivery of prescribed drugs.

## Consumer properties

- [CLINIC FOR](https://www.clinicfor.life/): Linc'well's clinic brand and patient-facing service.
- [CLINIC FOR shop](https://shop.clinicfor.life/): Cosmetics.
- [miteme lens shop](https://lens-shop.miteme.co.jp/): Contact lenses.
- [miteme drug shop](https://drug-shop.miteme.co.jp/): OTC medicines.
